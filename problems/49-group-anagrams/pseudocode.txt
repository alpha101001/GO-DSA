PROBLEM: 49. Group Anagrams
LINK: https://leetcode.com/problems/group-anagrams/description/
FUNCTION: func groupAnagrams(strs []string) [][]string

----------------------------------
--------------CODE----------------
----------------------------------
given words[], group <- map[string][]string

for wd in range := words:
    sorted = sort(wd)
    group[sorted].append(wd);

return group.values
----------------------------------
--------------CODE----------------
----------------------------------
